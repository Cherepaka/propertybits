Property Bits

Independent listed real estate research: dated decisions, financial models, model portfolio, public track record.

What we do

  • Research. Short, evidence-first notes on listed real estate in the US, UK, Singapore, Hong Kong, China and the UAE. We look for reasons to reject an idea before we look for reasons to buy it.
  • Model portfolio. Every decision is logged with date, price, thesis, expected return, kill condition, position size and review date. Past entries are never edited. We only add dated updates.
  • Screening and scoring. Our own framework filters a global universe down to a few candidates: hard gates first, then a scorecard.
  • Research projects. A limited number of paid analysis projects.

Founder

Slava Obodnikov leads Analytics & Research at Tranio Capital, a real estate private equity firm. He has 8 years in real estate, from civil engineering to private equity deals in Europe and the CIS, and about 5 years in investment analysis. His background includes Big 4 advisory, buy-side execution and public equity research.

Property Bits is his independent practice. The goal is an attributable, dated track record in listed real estate.

Track record
